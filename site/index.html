A price feed you can trust to say no.

BOT Chain has no Chainlink-style feeds. PriceWire reads the observation history inside BDEX V3 pools, applies health checks, and picks the deepest healthy tier, or refuses to answer.

The finding that shaped it

A live survey of WBOT/USDT on BOT Chain mainnet (5 Nov 2026):

The 0.05% pool prices WBOT about 27% above the deep 0.3% pool. A naive "read the first pool" integration would have quoted the wrong price. PriceWire prefers the deepest healthy tier and reverts NoHealthyPool if there is none. (Bars are indicative, not to scale.)

The contract

src/PriceWire.sol: stateless, no owner, no tokens, no admin. Immutable factory address.

FunctionWhat it does
consult(...)TWAP quote from one fee tier; reverts Unhealthy(flags).
consultBest(...)Quote from the deepest healthy tier of four; reverts NoHealthyPool.
health / healthAllNever revert; return flags and numbers for diagnostics.

Health flags: NO_POOL LOW_LIQUIDITY SHORT_HISTORY SPOT_DEVIATION LOCKED

Spot deviation means the spot tick is more than 200 ticks (about 2%) from the TWAP tick. The window is at least 60 seconds; use 30 minutes or more when money depends on it.

The Go service

Stdlib only. Reads the oracle with plain eth_call: no keys, no state changes. JSON with CORS and a 5-second cache.

GET /healthz
GET /v1/pairs
GET /v1/price?pair=WBOT-USDT&window=1800
GET /v1/health?pair=WBOT-USDT

/v1/price returns 503 with per-tier reasons when no tier is healthy, and 502 when the RPC itself fails, so an outage is never reported as "no healthy pool". /v1/health reports ok, degraded or down and warns when tiers disagree by more than 5%.

Risks and handling

Flash-loan push
TWAP over a window, plus a spot-vs-TWAP deviation flag.
Thin pools
minLiquidity is the caller's choice; consultBest prefers the deepest tier.
Young pools
SHORT_HISTORY when history is shorter than the window.
Residual risk
No on-chain cross-check between tiers (gas); the service warns instead.

Status. The contract and service are tested (9 unit/fuzz tests, 4 against live BDEX pools read-only, 10 Go tests), but PriceWire is not deployed. There is no address and no public API yet. Mainnet deploy soon. It is intended for projects like Dripbuy.
